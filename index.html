CPE 505

Computer Graphics and Animation

Computer graphics is the creation, storage, and manipulation of still or moving visual images and models using computers. Computer animation is a subfield of computer graphics that digitally generates the illusion of movement by displaying a sequence of slightly changed images over time. The course covers both 2D and 3D graphics and animation.
